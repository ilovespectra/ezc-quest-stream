1. Introduction
EZC ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our file management application ("the App").

2. Information We Collect
2.1 Information You Provide
Account Information: Email address, username, and authentication tokens when you sign up or log in

Device Information: Device name, operating system version, and device identifiers

File Metadata: File names, sizes, types, and paths of files you access through the App

2.2 Information We Collect Automatically
Usage Data: How you interact with the App, features used, and session duration

Performance Data: Crash reports, error logs, and performance metrics

Network Information: IP address, connection type, and bandwidth usage

2.3 Information from Third-Party Services
Google Drive: File lists, metadata, and access tokens when you connect your Google account

Send you service notifications and updates

Troubleshoot issues and fix bugs

Analyze usage patterns and trends

Ensure security and prevent fraud

Cloud Services: Files are accessed through third-party APIs and remain with their respective providers
